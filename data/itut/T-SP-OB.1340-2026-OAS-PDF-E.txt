        system for vehicular multimedia

–       ITU-T G.874 (04/2026): Management aspects of optical transport network elements
–       ITU-T H.430.9 (04/2026): An architectural framework for first-person transfer immersive live experience
–       ITU-T J.154 (04/2026): Functional requirements for IP broadcast services in cable television by using multiple-
        input-multiple-output (MIMO) in IMT-2020 system

        chain

        slicing in IMT-2020 networks and beyond
