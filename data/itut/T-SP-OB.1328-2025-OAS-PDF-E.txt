                         Approval and deletion of ITU-T Recommendations
Approved Recommendations:
By AAP-24, it was announced that the following ITU-T Recommendations were approved, in accordance with the
procedures outlined in Recommendation ITU-T A.8:
-        ITU-T G.9943 (10/2025): High speed fibre-based in-premises transceivers - network management
-        ITU-T G.9949 (10/2025): WLAN management control interface (WMCI) for in-premises network
-        ITU-T G.9975 (10/2025): Technical requirements of extended reality service over in-premises networks
-        ITU-T J.157 (10/2025): Functional Requirements for secondary distribution of digital television and audiovisual
         content to portable devices using the wireless local area network
-        ITU-T K.61 (10/2025): Guidance on measurement and numerical prediction of electromagnetic fields for
         compliance with human exposure limits for telecommunication installations
-        ITU-T L.1510 (10/2025): Environmental key performance indicators for digital infrastructure adapting to climate
         change
-        ITU-T P.1100 (10/2025): Narrowband hands-free communication in motor vehicles
-        ITU-T P.1110 (10/2025): Wideband hands-free communication in motor vehicles
-        ITU-T P.1120 (10/2025): Super-wideband and fullband hands-free communication in motor vehicles
-        ITU-T P.1199 (10/2025): Parametric object-recognition-ratio-estimation model for remote monitoring of
         surveillance video delivered from autonomous vehicle
-        ITU-T P.1204 (10/2025): Video quality assessment of streaming services over reliable transport for resolutions
         up to 4K
-        ITU-T P.1204.1 (10/2025): Video quality assessment of streaming services over reliable transport for resolutions
         up to 4K using metadata
-        ITU-T P.1204.2 (10/2025): Video quality assessment of streaming services over reliable transport for resolutions
         up to 4K using metadata and frame size/type information
-        ITU-T P.1321 (10/2025): Interactive test methods for subjective assessment of extended reality communications




Deleted Recommendations:
None
