                        Approval and deletion of ITU-T Recommendations
Approved Recommendations:
By AAP-39, it was announced that the following ITU-T Recommendations were approved, in accordance with the
procedures outlined in Recommendation ITU-T A.8:
–       ITU-T Y.4245 (06/2026): Requirements and capabilities of accessible service management using user accessibility
        preference profile for IoT services
–       ITU-T Y.4246 (06/2026): Accessibility requirements for metaverse services supporting Internet of things
–       ITU-T Y.4247 (06/2026): Requirements of smart city communication network system
–       ITU-T Y.4514 (06/2026): Functional architecture of network connectivity management in the Internet of things
–       ITU-T Y.4515 (06/2026): Functional framework of an Internet of things (IoT) platform for metaverse applications
–       ITU-T Y.4516 (06/2026): Functional architecture of the IoT-based energy optimization system in municipal
        lighting facilities
–       ITU-T Y.4517 (06/2026): Architecture of the digital twin system in power grid
–       ITU-T Y.4615 (06/2026): Artificial Intelligence of Things - On-device framework and requirements for robot
        services
–       ITU-T Y.4616 (06/2026): Framework of an integrated intelligent IoT service based on multi-edge computing
–       ITU-T Y.4618 (06/2026): Artificial Intelligence of Things - Reference model and requirements

their webpage concerning the national numbering plan as well as their contact points, so that the information, which
will be made available freely to all administrations/ROAs and service providers, can be posted on the ITU-T website.

are kindly requested to use the format as explained in Recommendation ITU-T E.129. They are reminded that they will
be responsible for the timely update of this information.
