China Telecommunications Corporation Satellite Communications Branch (country code +882 52)
Communication of 10.XII.2025:
China Telecommunications Corporation announces the following information about the E.164 shared Country Code
and associated Identification Code 882 52 assigned to China Telecommunications Corporation (China Telecom):
The Tiantong international numbering service is based on the Tiantong satellite mobile communication system operated
by China Telecom. It is a numbering resource (882 52 number segment, 13-digit number) assigned by the International
Telecommunication Union (ITU) for China Telecom to run global operations. Currently, it has the capability to provide
voice, SMS, and Internet of Things services over satellite to users in countries/geographic areas covered by the Tiantong
satellite network. China Telecommunications Corporation Satellite Communications Branch is the operator of this
business.

The usage division of the Tiantong international numbering plan is as below：

    Number Segment                        Country/Region                         Total length

                  0                            China                                  13
                  1                            China                                  13
                             HongKong, China; Macau, China;Taiwan,
                  2         China and 17 countries covered by Tiantong                13
  88252                         Satellite Systeme in Southeast Asia
                  3                           backup                                  13
                  4                          IoT users                                15
                 5-9                          backup                                  13

China Telecommunications Corporation Satellite Communications Branch has designated China Telecom Global Limited
to transmit international voice and SMS services sent to China Telecommunications Corporation Satellite
Communications Branch. China Telecommunications Corporation Satellite Communications Branch kindly requests
service providers to program their switches to support access to these number segments.
