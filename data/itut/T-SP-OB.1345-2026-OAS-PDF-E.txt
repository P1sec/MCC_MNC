Gambia (country code +220)
Communication of 03.VII.2026:
The Gambia Public Utilities Regulatory Authority (PURA), Serrekunda announces that with effect from 4th September
2026 at 00:00 hours, all AFRICELL, COMIUM and QCELL numbers in The Gambia shall be increased from seven (7) to
nine (9) digits.
With effect from 4th September 2026, the new dialing instruction of the closed numbering of nine (9) digits for AFRICELL,
COMIUM and QCELL mobile please use the following dialing procedures for calls within The Gambia:
    • QCELL: Dial 83 + the 7-digit subscriber number.
    • COMIUM: Dial 86 + the 7-digit subscriber number.
    • AFRICELL: Dial 87 + the 7-digit subscriber number.
The number length for change AFRICELL, COMIUM and QCELL shall be done in Phase 1.
The 7-digit numbering plan for GAMTEL and GAMCEL increase to 9-digits shall take effect in Phase 2, on a date to be
determined. Before that date, for GAMTEL and GAMCEL please use the following dialing procedures for calls within The
Gambia:
    • GAMCEL: Dial the existing 7-digit subscriber number.
    • GAMTEL: Dial the existing 7-digit subscriber number.
International dialing format: +220 XX XXX XXXX
Details of the changes (phase 1) to AFRICELL, QCELL and COMIUM mobile numbers:
